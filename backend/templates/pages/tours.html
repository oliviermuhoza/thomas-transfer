Full-day tour · Southern Province

Nyungwe National Park

One of Africa's oldest montane rainforests and a UNESCO World Heritage Site — home to chimpanzees, 13 primate species and East Africa's only canopy walkway, 60 metres above the forest floor.

  • 05:00Early departure from Kigali through tea country (±5 hrs, with scenic stops)
  • 10:30Igishigishigi trail & the famous Canopy Walkway
  • 13:00Lunch near Gisakura tea estate
  • 14:30Waterfall trail or colobus monkey tracking (chimp trekking available with overnight option)
  • 16:30Return drive — or extend to an overnight stay on request
$300$270 Per person
